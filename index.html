        Method transfer · equivalence · TOST

        Is the new lab the same as the old one?

        An ordinary t-test cannot answer that: failing to find a difference is not proof of sameness, it is often just too little data. TOST asks the right question — is the difference small enough to ignore? — and gives a yes, a no, or an honest "not yet".

        Results from both methods

        One row per result: which method or lab produced it, and the value. Add a sample identifier and the same samples can be matched up as pairs, which is far more sensitive. Learn how this works ↗

        TOST at 0.05 is the same statement as a 90% confidence interval sitting inside the margins. Welch is the safer default: it does not assume the two methods are equally precise.

        How your data stays private

        • Nothing is uploaded. Your file is read by your own browser. All math (models, DOE, charts) runs in JavaScript on your device.
        • The browser is told to block every outgoing connection. This page carries a security policy with connect-src 'none', so even a bug or a bad add-on in this code could not send your data anywhere.
        • Nothing is stored, and you can delete on demand. No cookies, no local storage, no database. Press Finish and delete my data when you are done. Data is also wiped automatically after 30 idle minutes, when you leave the page, or when you close or refresh the tab.
        • No AI service sees it. The agent is built from classic statistics and machine learning plus written rules. No LLM, no API key, no prompts sent anywhere.
        • No trackers. No analytics, ads, web fonts or third-party scripts.
        • Works offline. Once the page has loaded you can switch off Wi-Fi and it still works.

        Fine print: like any website, the host (GitHub Pages) sees that the page was opened. It never sees your data. Browser extensions you install can read any page, so use a clean browser profile for sensitive data.
